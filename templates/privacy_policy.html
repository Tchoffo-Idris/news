This Privacy Policy explains what information Vintage Editorial collects when you use our site, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or browsing our articles, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

  • Account information — your username, email address, and password when you sign up.
  • Content you submit — articles, comments, and edits you publish through your Editor's Desk.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, search queries, and general interaction with the site.

How We Use It

We use your information to operate your account, display your published work, personalize your reading experience, and keep the site secure. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember preferences such as dark mode. These are required for the site to function and cannot be disabled without affecting your experience.

Sharing Your Information

We share information only when required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our readership, or with service providers who help us operate the site under strict confidentiality obligations.

Your Rights

You can review, update, or delete your account information at any time from your account settings. You may also request a copy of the data we hold about you by contacting us directly.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us through your account's support channel and we'll respond as soon as we can.
