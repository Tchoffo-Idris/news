About Us

Vintage Editorial began as a small experiment in independent publishing — a place for writers to share dispatches, essays, and stories without the noise of a crowded newsfeed. What started as a handful of contributors has grown into a community of readers and correspondents who believe good writing still deserves a considered home.

What We Publish

Our pages carry reporting, opinion, and long-form storytelling from independent voices. We favor clarity over spectacle, and we'd rather run one well-considered piece than ten rushed ones.

Our Principles

  • Every contributor writes under their own name and stands behind their work.
  • We correct mistakes publicly and promptly.
  • Reader trust is earned one issue at a time, not assumed.

Get Involved

If you'd like to write for us, sign up for an account and submit a piece through your Editor's Desk. We read everything that comes through, and we're always glad to hear from new correspondents.

— The Vintage Editorial Team
